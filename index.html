Common questions

A few things to know.

How do I get the best result?

Use a clear, well-lit photo with one person visible. AI results vary and may change details of your appearance or outfit. Drape offers creative style inspiration; images do not guarantee a garment’s physical fit.

Where are my finished photos?

Open Fittings to view your tasks and save finished images to your device. Server results are cached for up to 7 days, so save any photos you want to keep.

How do gems and Drape Pro work?

Generation costs and any Pro requirement are shown before you submit a task. Available gem packs, subscriptions, prices, and included benefits are displayed in the app before purchase.

How do I restore purchases or manage a subscription?

Use Restore Purchases in the app’s purchase screen with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your iPhone’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions. For a refund request, visit Apple’s Report a Problem.

How do I delete my account?

In Drape, open You → Settings → Delete account and confirm. We delete or anonymize personal information within 30 days, except records we must retain by law. Deleting an account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage that separately in Apple’s subscription settings.

What happens to my uploaded photo?

It is processed to create your requested fitting and deleted after the task completes. Generated results are cached for up to 7 days. We do not use uploaded photos for public model training without your consent. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
