Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how ANTHONY DAVID HUFFMAN (“we,” “us”) handles information when you use Drape — AI Outfit Studio and contact Drape support.

1. Information we process

  • Account information: email address, account identifier, profile information you provide, and authentication information associated with email sign-in or Sign in with Apple.
  • Photos and creations: photos you choose to upload, their visible appearance (including faces), selected outfits, generation parameters, task status, and generated images.
  • App and device information: device identifiers and model, operating system and app version, service requests, diagnostic information, and activity associated with your account and generation tasks.
  • Purchases: Apple transaction references, purchase status, subscription entitlements, and credit balances. Apple processes payments; we do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Support messages: your email and information you choose to include when contacting us.

2. How information is used

We use this information to authenticate your account, process the fittings you request, deliver and display results, manage credits and purchases, restore entitlements, troubleshoot problems, respond to support requests, protect the service from misuse, and meet legal obligations.

Photo access lets you select a source image and save results. You control access through your device’s settings.

3. Photo processing and AI providers

When you start a fitting, your selected photo and task parameters are transmitted to our servers and AI processing providers to generate your requested outfit portrait. The service uses the person’s visible appearance to create the result.

Uploaded reference photos are deleted after task completion. We do not use your photos to train public models without your consent.

Only upload photos of yourself or people who have given you permission. You can choose not to upload a photo; generation features require a source photo.

4. Sharing and service providers

Cloud hosting, storage, and AI processing providers handle the information needed to operate the service. Apple handles Sign in with Apple and in-app purchases under its own policies. We may also disclose information where required by law or necessary to address fraud, abuse, or threats to safety.

Service providers may process information in countries other than where you live. Applicable data protection requirements continue to apply to that processing.

User and device identifiers are used for app functionality, including account access, task association, and purchase entitlements. We do not use them to track you across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising.

5. Retention and deletion

Information Retention
Uploaded reference photos Deleted after task completion
Generated results on our servers Cached for up to 7 days
Account information While your account remains active
Purchase and transaction records As required by applicable law

After account deletion, we delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except information we are legally required to retain. Images you save to your device remain under your control and can be deleted from your photo library.

6. Your choices and requests

You can delete your account in Drape under You → Settings → Delete account. To request access, correction, a copy, or deletion of your information, contact andahuf0928@icloud.com . We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

Manage photo permissions in device settings and Apple subscriptions in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Account deletion does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription.

7. Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal information. No online system is completely secure. Contact us if you believe your account or information has been compromised.

8. Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above identifies the latest version. We will provide additional notice where required by applicable law.

For privacy questions, contact ANTHONY DAVID HUFFMAN at andahuf0928@icloud.com .
